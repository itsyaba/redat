# M2 — daemon + hotkey + VAD (as built)

- `src/daemon.ts`: owns the mic, the Claude session and playback. States: idle → listening → thinking → speaking.
  Unix socket at `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/redat.sock`, one JSON line per request.
- `redat listen` (SUPER+Y) does different things depending on state: idle → listen; listening → stop
  recording now; speaking → cut the reply and listen again (barge-in); thinking → "busy" notification.
- `src/vad.ts`: energy VAD on raw PCM from `pw-record --raw`. The first 300ms set the noise floor;
  the threshold is max(80, 3.5×floor); speech starts after 150ms of voice and ends after 1.1s of silence;
  it gives up if nothing is said within 7s and caps a recording at 20s. Keeps 300ms before speech starts.
  Tuned on tmp/mictest.wav (my voice 200–2500 RMS, room ~0–25).
  Env overrides: REDAT_VAD_MIN, REDAT_VAD_SILENCE_MS.
- Earcons are generated sine WAVs in ~/.local/state/redat/sounds (listen / heard / error / cancel).
- One notify-send notification, updated in place: listening → transcript → tool command → reply.
  If TTS fails, the reply stays in the notification for 30s and the error chime plays.
- The conversation persists in the daemon; a new one starts after 15 min idle or with `redat reset`.
- Turn history: ~/.local/state/redat/turns.log (JSONL with timings).
- systemd: systemd/redat.service, symlinked into ~/.config/systemd/user, WantedBy graphical-session.target.
- Hyprland: a marked block in ~/.config/caelestia/hypr-user.lua (backup next to it, *.bak-redat-*).

Known limits: energy VAD triggers on other people talking nearby (the wake word in M4 fixes that).
While waiting on TTS the state is still "thinking", so the hotkey can't interrupt it yet (M3).
